## Unreleased

- Comments on every platform: `social_comment` also writes on X, Threads, Instagram, YouTube, Bilibili, Facebook and Rednote. Each adapter's `write` (`Writer`) names what a link replies to and sends the comment; X, Threads, YouTube, Facebook and Rednote by typing into the platform's own page (`compose.ts`), confirmed by the platform's answer to the request that carried the text. A link to a comment is refused where the bot replies to no comments.
- `social_post` writes new posts on X, Threads, Reddit (a text post in a subreddit, with a title) and Bilibili (a dynamic): drafts, or with `posts.auto` queued at least 60 minutes apart (`GAPS.post`). `posts.*` in the config; a New posts sheet on the panel, where the title of a post draft can be edited (`commentPost` takes it).
- Text limits follow each platform's own count (`lengthOn`, `limitOf`; X weighs links and CJK characters) and leave room for the automatic mark, which is in Chinese on Bilibili and Rednote.
- `social_comment` drafts comments on Reddit posts and comments, posted only from the panel (`commentPost`, `commentDiscard`); `social.comment` tells the bot what the person did. `comments.*` in the config.
- Automatic comments and automatic replies: the bot's comments, and its answers to new replies in the Reddit inbox (`social.reply`), go out on their own, queued and spaced (`FIRST_WAIT_MS`, `GAPS`); `social.posted` tells the bot when one went out or became a draft; the panel's `queueHold` keeps one back.
- Substack: `social_browse` reads the feed, a publication's archive, a person's Notes and posts (`@handle`, `me`) and a category's leaderboard; `social_read` reads a post with its comments; `social_comment` comments on posts; `social_note` writes Notes and replies to Notes; `social_draft` creates post drafts in `substack.publication`, never published. Daily Notes at random times (`social.write`). `substack.*` in the config.
